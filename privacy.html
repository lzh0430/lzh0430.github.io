Privacy policy — survival-desk worker

Last updated: 2026-10-18

survival-desk worker is a private backup tool used only by its owner.

What it accesses

It asks Google only for the drive.file permission. With that permission it can see and change only the files it uploaded itself. It cannot see any other file in the Google Drive.

What it does with data

It uploads the owner's own backup files to the owner's own Google Drive and reads them back when the owner restores a backup. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to anyone else. No analytics, no advertising.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
